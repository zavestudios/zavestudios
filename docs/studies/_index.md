A study is a focused exercise on one element, made to build technique rather
than to be the finished work. These are the primitives the platform is written
in, examined on their own.

[Architecture](../architecture/) shows these applied — a token moving through a
real gateway, a certificate issued to a real workload. These pages ask a
different question: how does the primitive itself work, independent of any one
system.

## Identity and Trust

Most of what follows is one subject. A token, the framework that carries it,
the identity layered on top, and the machine-to-machine counterpart. Read in
order, each leans on the one before it.

## Traffic and Boundaries

Where a request is accepted, where it is refused, and what sits between
services.

## Observability and Policy

How a system reports on itself, and how rules are evaluated against it.

## Cloud

Primitives that have no box on this platform but matter elsewhere. Kept
separate so nothing here implies a footprint that does not exist.
